Meet the Speakers

Access Day brings together four professionals who work with web accessibility every day. Get to know each speaker and find the talks and workshops they lead.

John Doe

Accessibility Consultant

John helps product teams apply accessibility guidelines to their websites. He enjoys making technical standards easier to understand.

Sessions

  • Understanding WCAG and the POUR Principles (Talk)
  • Running Audits with Lighthouse and WAVE (Workshop)
  • When to Use ARIA (Talk)

Jane Smith

Quality Assurance Engineer

Jane tests websites to find accessibility barriers before they reach users. She combines automated tools with manual checks in her daily work.

Sessions

  • Applying the POUR Principles to a Web Page (Workshop)
  • Testing Websites for Accessibility (Talk)

Alex Taylor

UX Researcher

Alex studies how people use assistive technologies to browse the web. She turns these findings into practical improvements for content and design.

Sessions

  • How People Use Assistive Technologies (Talk)
  • Improving Headings, Alt Text, Form Labels, and Link Text (Workshop)

Maria Garcia

Front-End Developer

Maria builds accessible interfaces with semantic HTML and ARIA. She regularly tests her work with a keyboard and a screen reader.

Sessions

  • Navigating a Website with a Screen Reader (Workshop)
  • Creating Accessible Content (Talk)
  • Adding ARIA Roles and Attributes to a Page (Workshop)
